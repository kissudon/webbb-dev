SHINCHAN

SHINCHAN is a very funny and naughty five-year-old boy who lives in Japan. Created by Yoshito Usui, the popular animated series follows his everyday adventures with his family, including his mother Misae, father Hiroshi, baby sister Himawari, and pet dog Shiro. He loves watching his favorite superhero show, Action Kamen, and makes everyone laugh with his silly antics.
